librarylibs/Uart/src/Uart.xtl

Uart: a serial port (UART: 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit) as X_eTaL step functions: a transmitter, a receiver, and the two looped back Import it with an alias of your choice: "ua:" u_se< "Uart". Put libs/Uart/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Uart/docs. Names with l: are exported; the others are private to this file.

A line idles at 1. A byte is sent as a frame of ten bits, each held for d clocks: a start bit (0), the eight data bits, least significant first, and a stop bit (1). d, the clocks per bit, is the clock rate over the baud rate: 234 for a 27 MHz Tang Nano at 115200 baud, 3 in the tests. It is the first argument of every step, bound once: u:t_x := ua:t_x 3, then s0 'u:t_x ck:r_un commands (Clocked's harness). Each step is pure, one clock's input in, the next state out.
