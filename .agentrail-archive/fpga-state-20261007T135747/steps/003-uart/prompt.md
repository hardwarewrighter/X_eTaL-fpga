A UART in the netlist: a receiver (start bit, 8 data bits, stop bit, sampled at the middle of each bit) and a transmitter, with the clock frequency and the baud rate as schedule data (27 MHz and 115200 for the Tang Nano 4K); the calculator framed between them: ASCII characters in (the k_eys mapping as a lookup), the display out as decimal ASCII digits and a newline when = is pressed. Simulated end to end in Rust: the bits of '12+34=' in, the bits of '46' out. Verilog pinned. Docs: the RTL README (the UART), the Calc page, plan. Gate; CHANGES; commit; push.
